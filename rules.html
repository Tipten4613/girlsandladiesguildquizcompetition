Competition Rules

Guidelines for the Girls and Ladies Guild / Boys Samuel Mission Quiz Competition.

1. Participation

  • Participants should select the correct archdeaconry when registering attendance.
  • Each participant should follow the competition instructions displayed on the website.
  • Participants should compete honestly and respectfully.

2. Competition Categories

The competition covers three areas of knowledge:

  • Bible: Biblical knowledge and understanding.
  • Anglicanism: Anglican faith, history, worship, and traditions.
  • Current Affairs: Current events and general knowledge.

3. Questions and Time Limit

  • Each question has a time limit of 30 seconds.
  • Read each question and all available options carefully.
  • Select your answer before the timer runs out.
  • When time expires, the question will be handled according to the website's timer settings.
  • Timers starts Immediately the question is clicked

4. Scoring System

  • A correct answer earns 5 marks.
  • An incorrect answer earns 0 marks.
  • Scores are recorded by the website.
  • The scoreboard displays scores for individual categories and the overall competition.

5. Question Selection

  • Follow the website's instructions to select a category, archdeaconry, and question number.
  • Once a question number has been used, it may become unavailable for selection again.
  • Check your selection carefully before proceeding.

6. Fair Play and Conduct

  • Do not deliberately interfere with another participant's attempt.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate scores or competition records.
  • Respect fellow participants, officials, and competition decisions.
  • Maintain the spirit of Christian fellowship, discipline, and sportsmanship.

Knowledge • Faith • Excellence

Participate honestly, answer carefully, and give your best throughout the competition.
