ABOUT THE COMPETITION

Knowledge, Faith and Fellowship

The Girls and Ladies Guild / Boys Samuel Mission Quiz Competition brings participants together to learn, test their knowledge, strengthen their faith, and encourage healthy competition.

Who We Are

The Diocese Quiz Competition is an organised knowledge-based competition for participating archdeaconries. It provides an opportunity for members of the Girls and Ladies Guild and Boys Samuel Mission to engage in learning, demonstrate their knowledge, and share in Christian fellowship.

Our Purpose

The competition is designed to encourage biblical learning, understanding of Anglicanism, awareness of current affairs, and respectful participation. It combines learning with teamwork, discipline, and friendly competition.

Competition Categories

  • Bible: Questions on biblical knowledge and understanding.
  • Anglicanism: Questions about Anglican faith, history, worship, and traditions.
  • Current Affairs: Questions covering current events and general knowledge.

Knowledge

Encouraging learning, curiosity, and understanding.

Faith

Supporting growth in biblical and Christian knowledge.

Excellence

Promoting preparation, discipline, and fair play.

How the Website Works

  • Participants record their attendance and select their competition category.
  • They follow the instructions to select an archdeaconry and question number.
  • Each question has a 30-second timer.
  • Correct answers earn 5 marks, while incorrect answers earn 0 marks.
  • Scores can be viewed on the category and overall scoreboards.

Learn Together. Grow in Faith. Strive for Excellence.

May the competition encourage knowledge, fellowship, integrity, and a spirit of healthy Christian competition.
